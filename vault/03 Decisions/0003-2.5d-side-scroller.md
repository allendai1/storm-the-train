# 0003 — 2.5D side-scroller on a locked axis

## Context
The game is inspired by a 2D mobile side-scroller (see [[Original Game]]). The
perspective affects nearly every system: the camera, character movement, aiming,
enemy AI, level building and controls on each platform.

## Decision
- The game uses 3D models with a camera locked side-on. Gameplay happens along a
  single axis that runs the length of the train, plus vertical movement for jumps
  and spin attacks.
- Players, enemies and projectiles are constrained to the gameplay plane. Depth (the
  axis toward the camera) is for scenery and parallax only.
- Original values convert at about 15 px = 1 stud. Treat these as a balance starting
  point only.

## Alternatives considered
- **3D third-person on wide train roofs.** More Roblox-native, but it loses the
  original's readability, and most of the original balance data would stop applying.
- **A hybrid with a shallow depth lane** (dodging in and out, as in a brawler). This is
  more complex to read and to build AI for. It could be revisited later.

## Consequences
- Movement needs a server-respected plane constraint for players and enemies, such
  as an AlignPosition, a planar constraint or custom controls. Exact implementation
  is decided when movement is built.
- Each client runs a custom camera controller. Mobile and gamepad controls are a
  simple left/right/jump/shoot set.
- Carriages are built as hand-placeable templates with the gameplay surface aligned
  to the axis. Set dressing can use the depth axis freely.
