# Title Ideas

> **Working title chosen (2026-10-13): Train Runner.** The ideas below are kept as
> alternatives in case it changes before launch. Before publishing under any name,
> search Roblox for existing experiences with that name, and run a basic trademark
> search.

The game is moving away from the original's "Storm the Train" (see
[[Game Design Document]]). Brainstormed options:

| Title | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Full Steam Squad** | Co-op squad plus train in three words; fits the comedy tone |
| **Express Assault** | Punchy action; "express" carries the train meaning |
| **Off the Rails** | A common phrase that suits the chaos, but probably taken on Roblox already |
| **Boxcar Brawl** | Comedic and alliterative; a bit melee-sounding for a shooter |
| **Rooftop Express** | Says exactly where the action happens |
| **Hijack Express** | Clear premise, though the enemies are the hijackers |
| **Last Stop Squad** | Comedy agents; the hostile train's "last stop" |
| **Rail Raiders** | Classic, but likely crowded |

Names that also need choosing:
- The agency, replacing the original's unnamed agency.
- The hub train, replacing the Eagle-7.
- The shopkeeper and surplus shop, replacing Chubwick.
- The agents, replacing Graham, Mason, Carrera and the rest. Placeholder names are
  proposed in [[Launch Agents]]: Dash Daring, Brick Broadsley, Turbo, Nana Nitro, Mimi
  the Mime and Zorp.
