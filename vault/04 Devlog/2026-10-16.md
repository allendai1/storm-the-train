# 2026-10-16

## Done
A cloud session (no Studio). We went through the design notes' open questions and
decided every one that doesn't need a playtest:
- **Checkpoint vote:** a tie means continue, and the 90 s AFK safeguard stays
  ([[Loop (M2)]]).
- **Mid-run join:** no. Run servers are locked to the party roster ([[Loop (M2)]]).
- **Weapon crates** only switch the weapon. No refill, heal or cooldown reset
  ([[First Playable (M1)]]).
- **Queue pads:** no locks and no AFK removal ([[Queue Pads]]).
- **Tutorial:** skippable from the start ([[Writing and Dialogue]] §6).
- **Barks:** text bubbles with a voiced grunt ([[Writing and Dialogue]] §1).
- **Hub guide:** keep the Quartermaster. The name comes in the naming pass.
- **Mimi's Invisible Bow:** a fixed 70 piercing shot after a 0.8 s draw, with no charge
  scaling ([[Launch Agents]]).
- **Run-end coins:** 1 coin per 25 points of personal score ([[Economy and Monetization]]).
- **Daily login streak:** yes, a 7-day coin streak with gems on day 7. Missing a day
  resets it ([[Economy and Monetization]]). `LoginStreak` was added to the schema v1
  template in [[0008-player-data-profilestore]].
- **Confirmed as built:** the proposals that M1 and M2 already implement. Those are the
  crate rules, the 1.5 s swap cooldown, the Mayhem meter rules, the gadget locks and swap
  rules, the mech's stomp, armour, eject and toughness rules, Air Strike boss damage,
  drone weapon numbers, personal coin pickups, and keeping coins on a wipe or cash-out.
  The numbers stay open to playtest tuning.
- **Agents:**
  - stats are fixed (no agent levels),
  - duplicates are allowed in a party,
  - support gadgets help every teammate in range,
  - **solo uses the original's −40% theme penalty**; co-op stays at −25%.
- **Enemies:**
  - `BERSERK_HEALTH` is a wind-up stagger threshold,
  - the proposed introduction order and co-op scaling stand,
  - **charges use the original's speeds and can be outrun**. The GDD's "jump, not
    outrun" rule was dropped. Studio to-do: Big Bart's `ChargeSpeed` 36 → 10.7.
- **Gadgets:**
  - 10 levels with 5 weapon tiers,
  - the Smasher Drone becomes a burst crowd-clearer,
  - the Air Strike is the safety valve,
  - **gem deploys** are added: 5 gems doubling per run, 1 per train
    ([[Gadgets and Mayhem]]).
- **Economy** ([[Economy and Monetization]]):
  - **Decided:**
    - every agent is earnable free, and paid power never hurts teammates,
    - missions, blueprints and gem sources (about 12 per hour), about 2.4 Robux per
      gem, and no Robux coin packs,
    - the surplus shop, the daily agent trial and agent gem prices 80/100/100/150,
    - free private servers.
  - **Boosters last one train**, as in the original, so each covers a leg's first
    train. All types are always on sale.
  - **Left open:** no paid random items, honest pricing, the Premium Payouts focus,
    Mayhem co-op balance, and run-end coins using boosted score.
- **Score and GDD:**
  - the anti-kill-steal rules,
  - touch-first controls with gamepad as a nice-to-have,
  - ragdolls fade after 3 s, with at most 12 at once.
- **Naming** ([[Title Ideas]]):
  - the title is **Train Mayhem**,
  - the agency is **ROBLOX Assault Team**. It needs a check against Roblox's naming
    guidelines before publishing,
  - the hub train is the **Falcon-8**,
  - the Wild West gang is **the Cowboys**,
  - the Quartermaster is called **Quill**,
  - the shop stays the **Surplus Shop**.
- **Agent names:** the starter is **Max Mayhem** (was Dash Daring) and the alien is
  **Gleep** (was Zorp). Brick, Turbo, Nana and Mimi stay placeholders, with shortlists in
  [[Title Ideas]]. The design notes use the new names; system notes keep the Studio IDs.

- **Code drafts** (not run yet; a cloud session can't reach Studio):
  - [[Session 7 PlayerDataService Code]]: ProfileStore saving, reviewed and fixed,
  - [[Session 12 Hub Code]]: the Falcon-8 greybox, queue pads, teleports and BACK TO HUB.
    New decisions: the hub is side-on, and PLAY AGAIN is replaced by BACK TO HUB.
- **The original's binaries are decompiled.** `libMainApp.so` went through Ghidra and
  `classes.dex`/`resources.arsc` through jadx. The findings are in
  [[Original Game - Combat Feel (Native Code)]]:
  - hitscan has no spread, and slow guns have ±30% fire jitter,
  - the shotgun is a ray plus a close blast,
  - knockback is divided by weight, and `ENEMY_DAMAGE_TIMER` is the flinch cooldown,
  - score and Mayhem are paid per point of damage, the combo window is about 1 s, and the
    player has 0.3 s of i-frames.

  The proposals are in [[Hit Feedback]].
- **The full decompile:** all 3,004 game functions (Ghidra), analysed in 4 areas. The
  results are written up as 4 reference notes (enemy AI, weapons and gadgets, player and
  camera, run structure and meta), with the proposals in [[From the Original - Proposals]].
  Corrections:
  - the original's guns have no auto-aim,
  - only enemy weapons have fire jitter,
  - lobs launch at 24–31°,
  - **Big Bart's original charge is 2.5× walk speed, and damage triggers it**,
  - boosters clear at every checkpoint.

  Three decisions were re-asked and settled:
  - **the charge runs at 2.5× walk** (Bart 26.7; the Studio to-do is now 36 → 26.7),
  - **our wind-up stagger stays** (a deliberate departure),
  - **boosters last until the next checkpoint**, a whole leg.
- **The proposals are reviewed.** The accepted feel items for Max and the built game are
  collected in [[Combat Polish]] (planned). The boss rules (McGraw's pattern, Ma Rattler's
  bursts, the arena) went into [[Wild West Enemies]]; the Defender and Air Strike rules into
  [[Gadgets and Mayhem]]; the magnet, daily-challenge rewards and event payouts into
  [[Economy and Monetization]]. The developer's priority: **a playable, refined first
  agent** before the other agents' and enemies' mechanics.

- **The roadmap changed: [[Feel and Look (Slice)]] comes first** (the developer: a
  great-feeling map, models and character feel before economy or events). Sessions 7, 12
  and 13 wait. Prepared for it in the cloud:
  - F1: a reference board and a one-page style sheet in [[References]],
  - F2: the [[Combat Polish Code]] draft, reviewed and fixed,
  - F3–F6: [[Feel and Look Asset Specs]] (21 Max animations, the enemy sets, the train
    kit, characters and props, parallax and lighting values, 30 effects, about 45 sounds,
    a build order and 16 open questions).

- **[[Background Kit]]:** a standard for every theme's scenery: six slots (Foreground,
  Track, Ground, Near, Mid, Far) with fixed depth bands, variant tiles and set-pieces, a
  validator, and a Studio plugin for building tiles next to the train
  ([[Background Kit Code]], [[0011-background-kit]] proposed). Its maths found that the
  built controller can run short of copies on wide phones (Scrub, Foreground); the new one
  centres its copies, which fixes that. A code review fixed undo, selection-safety and
  performance problems before it was committed as reviewed.
