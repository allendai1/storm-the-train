# 2026-10-16

## Done
A cloud session (no Studio). We went through the design notes' open questions and
decided every one that doesn't need a playtest:
- **Checkpoint vote:** a tie means continue, and the 90 s AFK safeguard stays
  ([[Loop (M2)]]).
- **Mid-run join:** no. Run servers are locked to the party roster ([[Loop (M2)]]).
- **Weapon crates** only switch the weapon. No refill, heal or cooldown reset
  ([[First Playable (M1)]]).
- **Queue pads:** no locks and no AFK removal ([[Queue Pads]]).
- **Tutorial:** skippable from the start ([[Writing and Dialogue]] §6).
- **Barks:** text bubbles with a voiced grunt ([[Writing and Dialogue]] §1).
- **Hub guide:** keep the Quartermaster. The name comes in the naming pass.
- **Mimi's Invisible Bow:** a fixed 70 piercing shot after a 0.8 s draw, with no charge
  scaling ([[Launch Agents]]).
- **Run-end coins:** 1 coin per 25 points of personal score ([[Economy and Monetization]]).
- **Daily login streak:** yes, a 7-day coin streak with gems on day 7. Missing a day
  resets it ([[Economy and Monetization]]). `LoginStreak` was added to the schema v1
  template in [[0008-player-data-profilestore]].
- **Confirmed as built:** the proposals that M1 and M2 already implement. Those are the
  crate rules, the 1.5 s swap cooldown, the Mayhem meter rules, the gadget locks and swap
  rules, the mech's stomp, armour, eject and toughness rules, Air Strike boss damage,
  drone weapon numbers, personal coin pickups, and keeping coins on a wipe or cash-out.
  The numbers stay open to playtest tuning.
- **Agents:**
  - stats are fixed (no agent levels),
  - duplicates are allowed in a party,
  - support gadgets help every teammate in range,
  - **solo uses the original's −40% theme penalty**; co-op stays at −25%.
- **Enemies:**
  - `BERSERK_HEALTH` is a wind-up stagger threshold,
  - the proposed introduction order and co-op scaling stand,
  - **charges use the original's speeds and can be outrun**. The GDD's "jump, not
    outrun" rule was dropped. Studio to-do: Big Bart's `ChargeSpeed` 36 → 10.7.
- **Gadgets:**
  - 10 levels with 5 weapon tiers,
  - the Smasher Drone becomes a burst crowd-clearer,
  - the Air Strike is the safety valve,
  - **gem deploys** are added: 5 gems doubling per run, 1 per train
    ([[Gadgets and Mayhem]]).
