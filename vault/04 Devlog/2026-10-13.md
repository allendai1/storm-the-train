## GDD decisions, round 3

- **Health:** each player has 100 HP. The active agent's toughness multiplies the damage that player takes, using the original's spread (0.20–0.76), so fast agents are fragile and slow agents are tanky.
- **Speed:** the original's agent speeds at 15 px = 1 stud, which gives 18–33 studs/s. Charges in [[Wild West Enemies]] were raised (Bart about 36, McGraw about 40) so they're jumped, not outrun.
- **Revives:** a downed player bleeds out over 15 s. Teammate revives (hold 2 s, back at 40% HP) are unlimited. Gem self-revives double in price each use. A wiped squad gets a 10 s window to gem-revive.
- **Run structure:** endless runs in legs of 3 trains, each train 6–8 carriages and about 2–3 min. The 3rd train of each leg is an event train, with a boss every 2nd leg. Difficulty ramps across the whole run.

## GDD decisions, round 4

- **Squad:** each player brings up to 3 agents, as in the original, sharing one health pool. Only the active agent is on screen.
- **Roster:** 6 agents at launch, 2 of them free.
- **Leaderboards:** co-op only, no PvP. Proposed boards are furthest train and best score, global and friends, split by party size.
- **Event trains:** non-boss event trains rotate at random with no repeats.

## Launch agents draft

Drafted [[Launch Agents]] (status: proposed). It covers six agents on the original's speed/toughness curve: Dash Daring (free starter, all-rounder), Brick Broadsley (free at the first checkpoint, tank), Turbo, Nana Nitro, Mimi the Mime and Zorp. Each paid agent gets +40% damage against one theme and −40% against another, so every theme has one specialist and one weak agent. The note also proposes a swap cooldown, allowing duplicate agents in a party, and co-op gadget healing.

## Launch agents accepted

The developer accepted the six [[Launch Agents]] concepts (names are placeholders) and the note moved to `status: planned`. Brick Broadsley is the second free agent. The theme penalty was softened from −40% to −25% for co-op, while the bonus stays at +40%. Nana and Ma Rattler both keep "Eat your vegetables!" as a running gag.

## Gadgets and Mayhem draft

Drafted [[Gadgets and Mayhem]] (status: proposed):
- a 0–100 Mayhem meter per player, filled by knockouts and assists, with a target of one full meter every 45–60 s,
- you choose 1 of your active agent's 3 gadgets when the meter is full,
- lock rules adapted from the original, and no paid deploys,
- the 6 drones; the Smasher was redesigned so it's still useful in the Wild West,
- the 3 mech suits, each with an entry stomp and its own armour bar, themed per agent (Zorp's Saucer, Brick's Heavyweight, Nana's Rocking Chair Mech),
- the Air Strike.

Durations are the original's values; damage, armour and fill numbers are new proposals. Also flagged that Swap and Interact share the E key in the GDD's controls table.

## Score draft and controls fix

Drafted [[Score and Combo]] (status: proposed): knockout points (light 10, mid 100, boss 1,000), a personal combo with tiers up to ×6, flat bonuses for trains, events, no-damage trains and revives, and the squad score (the sum of players) for the leaderboard. Fixed the PC controls clash: Interact moved to W / ↑, so E stays swap; on gamepad, Y repeats the last gadget.

## Leaderboard decisions

- There are three boards: furthest train, best squad score and best individual score.
- Boards rank unboosted score; score boosters only affect personal score and missions.
- Gem-revived runs count, since the doubling price limits them.

[[Score and Combo]] is now `status: planned`.

## Economy and monetization draft

Drafted [[Economy and Monetization]] (status: proposed). It covers:
- three currencies (coins, gems, blueprints) and personal loot in co-op,
- the original's upgrade ladder (51,400 coins per item, about 1.08 M to max the launch gear),
- blueprints banked at checkpoints and lost on a wipe,
- run-end coins from score (1 coin per 25 points),
- Robux gem packs at about 2.4 Robux per gem,
- boosters that last a leg, with damage and health caps in co-op,
- surplus shop pricing, daily challenges as agent trials,
- a Launch Squad pass, a starter pack and a cosmetic first-purchase gift,
- earn-time targets: a 100-gem agent free in about 8 hours, everything maxed in about 100 hours.

No paid random items.

## Economy decisions

[[Economy and Monetization]] is now `status: planned`:
- carried blueprints are lost on a wipe,
- the medbay heal is free,
- no ads,
- the Robux products are gem packs, the Starter Pack (199), the Launch Squad pass (799) and the Coin Bonus pass (399); the first-purchase skin was dropped.

## Final GDD batch: design settled

The developer reviewed every remaining **[proposed]** GDD section, and the GDD is now `status: planned`.
- **Accepted as written:**
  - the pitch and pillars,
  - the core loop and run anatomy,
  - key mechanics,
  - the enemy system,
  - train events,
  - matchmaking, solo scaling and the hub train (Exchange pod added),
  - milestones M0–M5,
  - the small details,
  - the [[Wild West Enemies]] roster (now `planned`).
- **Changed:**
  - Audience: **all ages**, with no specific target.
  - Platforms: **PC and mobile only**, no console. Gamepad stays a nice-to-have.
  - Knock-outs: **ragdolls that stay on the train**, then fade out, instead of stars, smoke or flying off. The Wild West, gadget and economy notes were updated to match.
  - The original's numbers are now **loose reference only**, not a balance baseline. Values the developer chose on purpose (speeds, toughness, HP, revive and run rules) stay.

Still open: naming, art direction and number tuning. Next is M0 Bootstrap in Studio from a local session.
